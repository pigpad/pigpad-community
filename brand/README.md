# pigpad brand

This folder holds **pigpad's own** logo and pig art, plus **generic card placeholders**, for press and community use (articles, reviews, community posts).

- Use the assets as provided; don't alter the logo's colors or proportions.
- Generic placeholders are plain card shapes with no issuer branding.

**No issuer card art, ever.** Bank and network card images are their owners' property and are never published here.
